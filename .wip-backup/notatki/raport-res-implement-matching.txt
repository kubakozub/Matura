WHAT CHANGED

app/src/main/java/com/matura/app/game/Matching.kt
- acceptedAnswers(cell): three additions. (1) Next to every variant, the same text with the apostrophe characters ' U+2019 U+2018 U+02BC ` deleted before normalisation ("can't stand" accepts "cant stand", "dry cleaner's" accepts "dry cleaners"). (2) A slash inside a phrase expands word by word into every combination ("szkoła żeńska/męska" accepts "szkola zenska" and "szkola meska"; "żonaty/zamężna" both words); a slash touching a space ("his / her") separates two whole answers; at most 16 combinations per phrase, taken in a fixed order. (3) Separators inside a closed bracket pair no longer cut the cell ("syndykat (związek, grupa)" accepts "syndykat" and the whole cell, not "grupa", not "syndykat zwiazek"). The no-space twin rule, the order (whole cell first) and the no-duplicates property are kept.
- New public fun samePrompt(a: String, b: String): Boolean, as specified (whole normalised cell plus parts split on top-level commas and semicolons, spaced slash, slash words expanded, glosses dropped). Two blank cells are never the same.
- answersForPrompt now unions the accepted answers of every row whose prompt side is the same question in that sense. KDoc updated.
- New private helpers: splitOutsideGlosses, alternatives (shared by answers and prompts), promptAlternatives, a private samePrompt overload that takes one side precomputed, constants APOSTROPHES, GLOSS, GAP/GAPS, SPACED_SLASH, MAX_SLASH_COMBINATIONS. normalize, isPrefixOfAny, isExactMatch are untouched.

app/src/main/java/com/matura/app/ui/TrueFalseScreen.kt (buildQuestion, hasFalseCandidate) and MultipleChoiceScreen.kt (buildRound): the three whole-cell comparisons (normalize(candPrompt) == promptKey) are now Matching.samePrompt(candPrompt, prompt); the unused promptKey locals are gone and the adjacent comments describe the new rule. Nothing else in those files changed; signatures are intact.

READINGS OF THE SPEC THAT YOU SHOULD KNOW ABOUT

1. Old slash fragments stay. The old rule cut at every slash, so "single-sex school" accepted the bare tail "meska". The pure-superset rule requires it, so it is still accepted (pinned by a test). It is NOT an alternative for samePrompt: "szkoła żeńska/męska" and "męska" are different questions.
2. Point 3 is implemented as a gloss-aware split, not as "delete glosses first": the part WITH its gloss text is still accepted ("syndykat zwiazek przedsiebiorstw"), because EngineTest pins that. The protection covers comma, semicolon AND slash inside brackets. The slash case goes one step beyond the literal exception: "przyjaciel (kolega/koleżanka)" now accepts "przyjaciel" and no longer accepts "kolezanka" or "przyjaciel kolega". Custom sets only; the catalogue has no brackets.
3. A bracket that never closes is not a gloss: such a cell splits exactly as before (same list, same order). This keeps old behaviour for typos like "syndykat (związek, grupa".
4. A slash with a space on only one side ("his/ her") is treated as a spaced slash (two answers), the conservative choice. A stray slash at a word edge or a double slash never makes a word optional.
5. Word gaps for slash expansion include the hard space and other Unicode separators, written explicitly as [\s\p{Z}] so the JVM tests and Android's regex engine agree.
6. samePrompt ignores the typing shortcuts (no apostrophe, no space): the prompt is read, not typed, so "we're"/"were" and "a head"/"ahead" stay different questions.
7. The relation is not transitive and answersForPrompt takes the direct union only: "uczeń" gets the answers of "uczeń, student", but not of a third row that shares only "student".

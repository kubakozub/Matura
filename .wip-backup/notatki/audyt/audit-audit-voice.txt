SUMMARY: The shipped voice path is one class, VoiceInput (app/src/main/java/com/matura/app/ui/VoiceInput.kt, 2089 lines), a wrapper over android.speech.SpeechRecognizer that keeps listening by restarting after every utterance. escalate() holds a ladder of engines: the default engine, the same without EXTRA_PREFER_OFFLINE, the language tag without region, com.google.android.tts by name, every RecognitionService by ComponentName, and on-device recognition last. Listeners and timers are tied to a session generation (pokolenie); four timeouts (limitStartu, watchdog 6 s, straznikDzwieku 1.2 s, straznikCyklu 15 s) end silent engines; sprawdzModel asks checkRecognitionSupport (API 33+) whether an offline model exists. Pure rules sit in VoicePolicy.kt (VoicePolicy.path/label, SilenceDetector, eskalowacPoPustym, reakcjaNaBlad, zaliczycWypowiedz, policzStrony, Wznowienie, VoiceLog, stanSesji). game/VoiceMatch.kt fuzzy-matches transcripts to the answers on the board. PlayScreen.kt creates the engine with rememberVoiceInput(context) (line 318), chooses pl-PL or en-US in answeringPolish(), turns results into engine.type + submit in onHeard, and on onExhausted/onBrakZgody falls back to the system ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH dialog (oknoSystemowe) or the phone keyboard; VoiceBar draws the button and one status line. The offline variant (app/src/vosk/java: VoskVoiceInput 938 lines, VoskGrammar 244) records with AudioRecord on its own thread and decodes with Vosk 0.3.75 against a word grammar; app/build.gradle.kts adds its sources, models (src/vosk-assets), ABI filter and dependency only with -Pvosk=true, and nothing calls it. tools/vosk-fetch.sh, vosk-slim.py and vosk-models.sha256 rebuild the models; vosk-oov.py lists entries the model cannot recognise. Tests: Robolectric suites driving ShadowSpeechRecognizer (VoiceDrabinkaTest, VoiceModelOnDeviceTest, VoiceRegresjaTest, VoiceWalidacjaTest), pure rule tests, VoiceMatchTest (55 synthetic cases), Compose tests in testDebug, and VoskGrammarTest (grammar only, no audio).

INTEGRATION: RECOMMENDATION: do not wire the offline (Vosk) engine in this release. Build the Play bundle without -Pvosk=true (the default) and treat wiring as a separate project that starts with a device test. Reasons are in section 7. Confirmed with grep on HEAD, origin/master and origin/publikacja: rememberVoskVoiceInput and VoskVoiceInput( have no call site, so a -Pvosk=true build carries models, libvosk and JNA that nothing reaches.

1. What PlayScreen uses from rememberVoiceInput(context). There is no interface; the type is the concrete class VoiceInput. 26 members:
- read: listening, mode, status, oknoDostepne (Compose state); maSilnik, stan (plain getters); wypowiedz
- written: tekstCiszy, tekstInnySilnik, tekstBrakMowy, tekstBrakSieci, tekstBrakPakietuJezyka, tekstBrakPakietu, tekstSilnikMilczy, tekstGlosNiedostepny, tekstJezykPolski, tekstJezykAngielski, tekstBrakZgody, tekstBrakModeluOnDevice (PlayScreen.kt:437-456); onExhausted, onBrakZgody (699-703)
- called: start(language, biasing, kierunek, onHeard) (423); stop(PowodStop) with DOTKNIECIE, PAUZA, TLO (664, 707, 720); przelaczJezyk(tag): Boolean (480, 482); refresh() (667); diagnostyka() (750, debug only); release() from rememberVoiceInput.
VoiceBar takes no engine object, only values: listening, mode, message, str, onToggle, onDiagnostyka, dialogAvailable, hasEngine.
Not used by PlayScreen: brakModeluDla, onBrakModelu, ostatniBlad, probowane, silnikOpis, probe().

2. Does VoskVoiceInput expose the same surface? 24 of 26 members match. Two compile breaks: stop() has no PowodStop parameter (VoskVoiceInput.kt:285) and tekstBrakModeluOnDevice does not exist. Members with no caller at all: poziomGlosu, ustawSlownik(), przygotujWTle(), tekstPrzygotowanie, tekstBrakModelu, tekstMalaPamiec, tekstPrzelacznik. Behaviour differences: biasing is a closed grammar, not a hint; without models in the package maSilnik is false, so replacing the system engine outright would send every tap to the system dialog; mode is only ON_DEVICE or UNAVAILABLE; its wypowiedz counter starts at 0 on its own.

3. Where the switch belongs. PlayScreen.kt is in src/main and is compiled in both variants, so it cannot name VoskVoiceInput. Needed: (a) an interface in main with the 26 members, implemented by VoiceInput; (b) one factory function with two bodies in mutually exclusive source directories: a new app/src/novosk/java/.../GlosOffline.kt returning null and app/src/vosk/java/.../GlosOffline.kt returning VoskVoiceInput. In app/build.gradle.kts replace line 41 by main.java.srcDir(if (zModelamiOffline) "src/vosk/java" else "src/novosk/java") outside the if-block, and add a buildConfigField boolean to gate the OPTIONS row. CI already compiles both variants (ci.yml line 51), which keeps this seam checked. Class.forName would also work today because R8 is off, but it fails silently after a rename.

4. Options and strings. Strings.kt:143-182 defines speechEngine, speechEngineAuto/System/Offline, three *Desc texts, voskPreparing, voskUnavailable, voskLowMemory, micPrivacyToggle. None is referenced anywhere. Options (Models.kt:139-156) has no engine field; there is no enum, no row in OptionsScreen (Screens.kt:541-657), nothing persisted. The AUTOMATIC mode promised by speechEngineAutoDesc has no implementation: it needs a wrapper that starts the offline engine from onExhausted before PlayScreen.awaryjnie(). Adding a field with a default to Options is safe for old saves (Repository Json has ignoreUnknownKeys and encodeDefaults); keep the enum in main in every build.

5. Defects inside the offline code to fix before any wiring:
- Partial results skip the confidence gate: every partial that is not [unk] goes to onHeard (VoskVoiceInput.kt:492-496, 539-544); in grammar mode a partial is always an exact set word, so PlayScreen accepts it at score 1.0 (PlayScreen.kt:402). PROG_PL/PROG_EN then protect nothing. Simplest: deliver finals only.
- The grammar is the whole set (PlayScreen.biasingWords) while the board holds at most 5 monsters and 2 crates; a recognised off-board word reaches VoiceMatch, which accepts near neighbours (finding voicematch-accepts-near-neighbours). Fix that first.
- otworzMikrofon() reports failures with the current pokolenie instead of the thread's own number (lines 576, 598, 603): a dying thread can mark a newer session exhausted.
- stop() joins the audio thread on the main thread for up to 120 ms on every pause, background and toggle; while a model is loading the microphone stays open after stop.
- The constructor loads libvosk through JNA on the main thread during composition.
- Two engines in one round need one shared utterance counter, otherwise zaliczycWypowiedz can drop a legitimate answer.
- The four offline texts are never fed from Str, so an English interface would show Polish defaults.
- VoskGrammar keeps bracketed glosses that Matching.acceptedAnswers treats as optional; such custom entries cannot be said (built-in sets have none).
- Until 2 Nov 2026 (commit 1d8d19a) zapiszWDzienniku recursed and start() threw StackOverflowError, so this class has no history of a working run on a phone. Unit tests cover only VoskGrammar and two pure functions.

6. Minimal wiring plan, in order: (1) fix voicematch-accepts-near-neighbours and one-utterance-two-kills; (2) interface plus override in VoiceInput; (3) VoskVoiceInput implements it: stop(powod), tekstBrakModeluOnDevice, no partials, lazy native load, generation fix; (4) the two-directory factory and the BuildConfig flag; (5) SpeechEngine enum and Options field with default SYSTEM, OPTIONS row shown only when the flag is true; (6) PlayScreen picks the engine once per screen: SYSTEM gives today's object, OFFLINE gives the offline one if maSilnik else the system one; leave AUTOMATIC out of the first version; (7) reword the in-app privacy policy (Strings.kt POLITYKA_*: 'Aplikacja dostaje sam tekst' is false when the app itself records and decodes), the manifest comment and the Play data-safety answers, and add an in-app notice for the Apache-2.0 components (NOTICE exists only in the repo); (8) repair tools/vosk-oov.py and review its list; (9) publish to an internal or closed track first.

7. Risk, and why not now.
- Size and reach: about 17 MB grows to about 100 MB for every user (comment in app/build.gradle.kts), plus the unpacked model in filesDir; the ABI filter (arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a) removes x86 and x86_64 devices from Play if that build is published.
- Cannot be verified without a real device: AudioRecord behaviour (VOICE_RECOGNITION source, 16 kHz); CPU and memory of decoding next to the frame loop on 32-bit and low-RAM phones (about 113 MB resident for Polish per the code comment, guarded only by isLowRamDevice); first-run unpack time and storage; false accept and reject rates with real students and room noise (the thresholds come from synthetic recordings with at most 1.5 s of silence); game sound effects feeding into the microphone; another app holding the microphone; privacy-toggle detection through AppOps; native loading on 16 KB-page devices. On paper the last one is fine: in the Gradle cache libvosk.so 0.3.75 and libjnidispatch.so 5.18.1 for arm64-v8a and x86_64 have LOAD alignment 0x4000.
- Benefit is narrow: the system path already recovered the phone from the bug report (comment at VoiceInput.kt:197-216); the offline engine helps only phones where every system service fails.

8. Smaller shipped-path points not in the findings list:
- przelaczJezyk keeps preferOffline from the previous language (VoiceInput.kt:1634-1668; start() recomputes it at 1542) and the last ladder step requests offline when the model is merely unknown (1246-1250). Both contradict the policy sentence at Strings.kt:573; the ladder recovers by itself.
- stop(PowodStop.MIKROFON) after six ERROR_AUDIO (VoiceInput.kt:1050) ends listening without onExhausted, so the round keeps running, unlike every other failure.
- PlayScreen reads voice.wypowiedz when the result arrives (line 431), so a final delivered after a restart gets the new number, contrary to VoiceInput.kt:299-303.
- A model probe replaced by zwolnijSonde() leaves its 1.5 s limit armed, which later stores 'model missing' (1710-1742); stop() during the background probe chain ends it for the object's lifetime (sondaWTleZlecona).
- ostatniBlad is never reset in start(); a stale 12 or 13 can trigger the region-less tag step.

- maSilnik and stan read non-snapshot fields; the label follows them only because PlayScreen recomposes every frame. Keep that in mind when extracting the interface.
Outside my area, seen in passing: the in-app policy gives kuba1k@gmail.com (Strings.kt:582, 620) while docs/prywatnosc.html:74 and the store listing give jakubkozub1@gmail.com.

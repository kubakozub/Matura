SUMMARY: CLASSIC is one Android-free class, app/src/main/java/com/matura/app/game/GameEngine.kt (806 lines). ui/PlayScreen.kt owns it: `remember(set.id, restarts, lives, direction, speed) { GameEngine(set, GameConfig(lives, direction, speed, progress, today)) }`, a frame loop calling `tick(dt<=0.05)` and `snapshot()`, input through `type/backspace/setTyped/submit()`, and `takeLearningEvents()` after each frame and each submit; every `LearningEvent` becomes `Attempt(PRZYPOMNIENIE)` -> `Repository.recordAttempt` -> `Srs.applyTo`. The field is a unit square with the cannon at (0.5,0.5); monsters enter at x=-0.04/1.04 on one of five LANES (max 5) and walk straight to the cannon (radius 0.06). `level = 1 + kills/8` drives speed, spawn interval (3.8 s down to 1.4 s) and armour chance (level 3+, max 60%); crates (level 2+, every 19 s, TTL 13 s) grant 3 heavy rounds; multiplier x2/x3/x5 at streak 3/6/9; score += (10+2*level)*multiplier; a monster reaching the cannon costs a life and lives<=0 is the only end condition. `buildPlayable()` turns each Entry into `Playable(entryId, direction, prompt, answers)` (RANDOM = coin flip per row). `buildPlan()` orders them from `GameConfig.progress`: overdue by `potrzeba()`, up to `newWordsPerRound` new, not-due, remaining new; `nextTarget()` walks that list cyclically, or draws uniformly when progress is empty (board label REKORD instead of NAUKA). Movement, projectiles, armour, crates, lives, level curve and scoring behave as tested; the defects found are in word selection and answer matching.

HOOKS FOR THE PLANNED WORK (review queue, answer reveal, review list, self-ending session)
- The plan is built once in the constructor (326) and never changes; a word missed during the round returns only after a full cycle.
- Set-entry order is discarded by `playable.shuffled(random)` (342) and by the uniform draw, so App.kt:276 putting the practised hard word first has no effect. GameConfig has no field to pin entries to the front.
- `newWordsPerRound` only inserts the not-due words after the first 10 new ones. For a set with no known words the plan is simply all words shuffled, so the cap changes nothing.
- Crates take the next plan word (735); if the crate expires that word is skipped for the cycle and no event is written.
- The only end condition is lives <= 0 (665-668) and difficulty keeps rising with kills, so a 9-life session round can only end in KONIEC GRY or by backing out. A self-ending round needs a goal in GameConfig plus a snapshot flag. `tick()` returns early once gameOver is set (568), so corpses and flashes stop ageing.
- Reached monsters are removed without a Corpse (659). A reveal or review entry needs `solution` on Playable (answer side `.trim()` in buildPlayable 291-296) and a snapshot entity aged like corpses (580-581).
- Typed text is capped at 40 characters (427, 454).
- The engine is not thread-safe; all current callers are on the main thread (the Vosk variant posts through a Handler).

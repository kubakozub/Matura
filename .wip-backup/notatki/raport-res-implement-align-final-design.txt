WHERE I DID NOT FOLLOW THE FINAL DESIGN, AND WHY

1. recordGame parameter order and one detail of its meaning follow the DECISION, not the design text. The design has nowMs third and says bestStreak still moves when doRankingu is false; the decision puts doRankingu third and says bestStreak is not raised. Because my parameters must have defaults, nowMs defaults to System.currentTimeMillis(): the only place in Repository where a clock is not passed in. Tests pass time explicitly.
2. ui/App.kt. The draft had changed one line there (passing the time as third argument). With doRankingu third that line no longer compiles, so I restored the file to 2b3377e instead of editing it. Net effect: zero diff under ui/ against the base, and the App call uses the defaults (ranked, current time, no confirmation) until the screens team wires it.
3. naWplyw is the last parameter of recordAttempt, as designed. A side effect: a session trace can no longer be passed as a trailing lambda. One existing test call changed form for this (PostepIntegracjaTest.odpowiedzWSesji). Any test in the other branch that uses a trailing lambda there will fail to compile after the merge until the lambda is moved inside the parentheses.
4. seriaCzeka returns Int (0, 1, 2), not Boolean. C2 gives the boolean formula, C4 needs the number of bridged days for its two sentences; greater than 0 is the boolean.
5. Odliczanie is a sealed interface with Dzis, Jutro, Sto, Dni(ile) instead of DZIS, JUTRO, STO, DNI(d), and it carries the eight strings of the C3 table (stage convention: texts live on the types I add). It sits in Kalendarz.kt; the function is Nagrody.odliczanie as designed.
6. PacingTest does not assert "session-only reaches stage 2 on day 1". With the design's own student model (five new words at 60 percent) day one reaches 30 XP only when three of five are known: 8 of 12 seeds in my runs, day 2 in the rest. I assert "by day 2" for eight seeds. Everything else is asserted as written (stage 6 by day 25, no wait above 45 days, never stage 13, goal met on at least 160 days, 25 a day reaches stage 13 between day 50 and 100). The real Srs, Sesja.zaplanuj and Nagrody reproduce the design's simulated table closely: session only 12 500 to 12 700 XP, longest wait 33 to 37 days, stage 13 on day 70 to 73 at 25 a day and day 24 to 26 at 50 a day. The 50 a day bounds (day 15 to 40, 8 to 25 laps) are mine; the design gives none.
7. Names of test classes: DorobekIntegracjaTest is kept (design: DorobekZapisTest), and the font guard lives in OdznakiTest (design: CzcionkaPikselowaTest) because GryStr does not exist yet. It covers every Etap title, every Odznaka title, group labels and countdown strings.

KNOWN LIMITS (by design, documented in KDoc)
- A deliberately toggled clock can count one calendar day twice, and with a clock moved back and forth a word can be credited twice in one day (5 XP each; the first-climb premiums can never be taken twice, the property test covers both cases).
- Lowering the goal below today's points counts the day only at the next credit; until then the ring shows at most three quarters.
- A correct choice answer on a due box 1 word still moves its due date by a day, so typing it the same day earns nothing.
- Day, streak and review counters also grow on own sets (within the cap of 10 paid credits a day); word, set, topic and repair badges are catalogue only.

THE API, FOR WIRING

decide(transcripts: List<String>, board: List<Target>, others: List<Target>, polish: Boolean, isFinal: Boolean): Decision
- board: one Target per monster and per crate, id = Monster.id or Crate.id (one shared counter in GameEngine), answers = its answers list.
- others: the rows of the set, answer side in the listening language, answers = Matching.acceptedAnswers(side). Passing ALL rows is fine: a row that is also on the board changes nothing (tested). Their ids are not read. Build this list once per set and pass the same instance: Target caches the scorer forms of its answers.
- Decision(reason, answer, targetId, score, nearest, rival), accepted = answer != null. answer is the string to type, always in the spelling of the set, and non-null only for a shot. targetId is the first board target that accepts it. nearest and rival are for the debug overlay.
- Reason: EXACT and NEAREST are shots. OTHER_EXACT (the utterance is a word of a row that is not on the board), OTHER_NEAREST, TOO_FAR, AMBIGUOUS, NOTHING are rejections. Every partial result that does not fire is WAIT.

Recipe:
  val haslaPolskie = remember(set) { stronaPolska(haslo = true, set.termLanguage, set.definitionLanguage) }
  val tlumaczeniaPolskie = remember(set) { stronaPolska(haslo = false, set.termLanguage, set.definitionLanguage) }
  wierszePolskie / wierszeAngielskie = remember(set): for each entry, the sides whose flag is true / false, mapped to VoiceMatch.Target(0, Matching.acceptedAnswers(side))
  polskieOdpowiedzi / angielskieOdpowiedzi = the answers of those two lists as sets (this replaces the per-column sets)
  answeringPolish(): DEF_TO_TERM -> haslaPolskie, TERM_TO_DEF -> tlumaczeniaPolskie, RANDOM unchanged
  onHeard: keep the paused, gameOver and empty-board returns, then
    val board = live.monsters.map { VoiceMatch.Target(it.id, it.answers) } + live.crates.map { VoiceMatch.Target(it.id, it.answers) }
    val d = VoiceMatch.decide(alternatives, board, if (jezykNasluchuPolski) wierszePolskie else wierszeAngielskie, jezykNasluchuPolski, isFinal)
    val answer = d.answer; if (answer == null) { if (isFinal) voiceMsg = the quoted transcript; return }
    then zaliczycWypowiedz, type and submit exactly as today.
The "score < 0.9" line for partials goes away: decide() does it.

RULES AS IMPLEMENTED, AND WHERE I WENT BEYOND OR AWAY FROM THE BRIEF

1. Whole-utterance claim: as specified (whole form, then joined form, alternatives in order, board before others).
2. Fuzzy over board and others, winner must be on the board. Deviation: the rival is "the best answer that some board target accepting the winner does not accept", not "an answer of a target that does not accept the winner". The two agree on twins, second variants and shared answers. Mine also treats as harmless an answer that an off-board row shares with the target being shot ("staffroom" for "staff room", v036), which matches rule 1, and it is stricter when two board targets share the winner but only one accepts the rival.
3. Negation: the brief asked for exact prefix pairs. I found that this leaves "nieodpowiedzialna" hitting "odpowiedzialny" (0.77), "dojrzali" hitting "niedojrzały", "matura" hitting "immature" (0.88) when the other polarity is not in the set, so the rule is "closer without the prefix than with it". "imitation" for "invitation" and "niedojrzali" for "niedojrzały" still match. Also beyond the brief: inside decide() the part of an utterance that follows a standalone negation word is not read on its own, so "nie lubić" or "nie lubik" cannot hit "lubić". Cost: "in summer" cannot hit "summer".
4. Exact ties go to the longest answer.
5. Partial: 0.9 and no shot while the answer is a proper prefix or whole-word part of an answer of another target. Deviation: a longer variant of the SAME target does not delay ("syndykat" with a gloss cell).
Added because the sweeps demanded it:
- An utterance of several words is read as a whole first. Parts are not rivals of a whole reading ("zwolnjenie lekarskie" was blocked by the row "zwolnienie"). A word that is part of a longer answer of another target gives way when the rest of the utterance resembles the rest of that answer at 0.6 or more: "bluza sportowe" is "bluza sportowa", "low school" is "law school", but "the school", "no lubić", "kelner ta" stay "school", "lubić", "kelner".
- decide() reads parts of up to four words, not only words and pairs: "the mother in law" used to lose to the pair "in law" and hit "in-laws".
- Scores that cannot change a decision (length ratio under 0.6) are not computed and appear as 0 in Decision.score.
B. Spelling: -re by pattern after t, b, g, h (not macabre, timbre); -ise, -isation by pattern with a list of words that never take z (exercise, promise, surprise, advertise, -wise, -vise...); -yse, -ogue by pattern; -our and doubled l from stem lists, because a pattern would also touch flour, detour, bestseller, filled. Irregular list as in the brief plus licence, defence, offence, pretence, storeys, pyjama and practise/practised/practising (practise is in v076 and is not on your list). Plurals in -s of the irregular words follow. When "check" and "cheque" are both on the board the heard spelling wins; when only "cheque" is on the board and "check" is another row, "check" shoots "cheque" (otherwise it could never be said).
C. The empty ostatniaOdpowiedz means "nothing scored yet", so an engine that numbers utterances from 0 (the offline one) does not lose its first answer.
E. Clearing only when listening was true is my addition: after the ladder ends, a later stop(PAUZA) from the screen must not erase the message.

WHAT A PLAYER CAN NOTICE

Already now, with PlayScreen as it is:
- One spoken answer gives one credit even when the final result names a different answer than the partial. Side effect: two answers said in one breath give one credit; the second must be said after a pause.
- "grey" (v017) and "cosy" (v021) can now be said at all; the other 16 British terms match exactly instead of loosely, so partial results fire for them.
- A word no longer hits its negation or the reverse ("mature" and "immature", "dojrzały" and "niedojrzały"), also with a changed ending.
- The line under the voice bar is empty after the student switches listening off, pauses, or leaves the app.
After decide() and stronaPolska are wired:
- A word of the set whose row is not on the board never hits a neighbour ("shirt" for "skirt", "stopa" for "stopy", "boxer shorts" for "shorts").
- Near misses of answers with a space, hyphen or apostrophe are accepted.
- A partial result waits when the answer may still grow ("bluza" with "bluza sportowa" in the set).
- A custom set with swapped columns is heard in the language of its answers.
